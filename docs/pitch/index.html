04 · The solution
CargoFlow in one sentence

A financier escrows USDG per shipment; tranches reach the exporter only when the cargo's signed sensor evidence satisfies an on-chain policy; the buyer's one payment repays the financier, pays the exporter and hands over the bill of lading.

PHYSICAL REALITY → CRYPTOGRAPHIC EVIDENCE → EVIDENCE CONFIDENCE → FINANCIAL RISK → AVAILABLE CAPITAL → USDG SETTLEMENT

05 · How it works
Five beats · reference numbers

From escrow to settlement, every step on chain.

1

Escrow

Meera registers the shipment and a commit-reveal cold-chain policy (2 to 8 °C, humidity, shock). Daniel deposits the whole 40,000 USDG facility into the vault.

createFacility · depositCapital
2

Evidence

The logger signs each reading. Eight per sensor close an epoch: probes fused (Dempster-Shafer), conflict measured, scored 0 to 100. Only a salted Poseidon root and the score go on chain.

commitEpoch
3

Release

Evidence passes the policy in the right place: 8,000 USDG goes to Meera. The contract re-checks the committed epoch itself, whatever the backend asked for.

evaluateAndReleaseMilestone
4

Pause, then prove

Probe-1 hits 11.7 °C, conflict 74.8%, score 48: releases stop. A Groth16 proof that eight hidden readings of the other probe are in band, bound to this pause, resumes it.

pauseFinancing · resumeWithProof
5

Settle

Wei Lin pays 100,000 USDG once: 40,000 principal + 1,200 fee to Daniel, 58,800 to Meera, and the electronic bill of lading to her, in the same transaction.

markDelivered · settle

Title moves with the money

The carrier's ERC-721 bill of lading leaves escrow in the buyer's payment transaction.

Default and parametric cover

Pays after N failed epochs in a row, proven from the registry's commit order.

Recovery is automatic

The proof is prepared once a probe has eight fresh in-band readings; the exporter signs once.

Reference numbers 100,000 USDG invoice · 40,000 USDG facility in 5 milestones of 8,000 · 3% fee. The live testnet runs use the same mechanics at 30 / 20 USDG. Disputes go to an arbiter role that can never release money.

07 · Technology and moat
Technology and moat

Evidence, proof, money and title enforce each other.

Evidence engine

Dempster-Shafer fusion across sensors, conflict taken at the worst step (an excursion cannot be averaged away), seven capped penalties, a published 0 to 100 formula. Same input, same score.

Zero-knowledge recovery

Groth16, 13,494 constraints, about 1 s to prove, about 0.25 M gas to verify. Bound to chain, controller, shipment, epoch, policy, submitter and pause count: no replay.

Contracts hold authority

Immutable core (no proxy); eight invariants fuzz-tested; the pause brake stops new risk only, never exits. The AI monitor can only make an outcome stricter, through a pause-only key.

Device trust

Ed25519, P-256 secure elements (X.509 chain to manufacturer roots) or passkeys. Device class weights reliability; every epoch records its source devices on chain.

Electronic bill of lading

ERC-721 title per bill, held in escrow and released in the buyer's payment transaction: documents against payment. Designed around MLETR concepts, not a legal claim.

Parametric cover

Pays after N consecutive failed epochs, proven from the EvidenceRegistry's commit order: principal to the financier, salvage to the exporter. Pull-based payouts.

Hard to copy Not one feature but the chain of them: signed device readings, a fusion score, a circuit bound to the contract's own public signals, a vault that only the controller can release, and a title registry that moves with the payment. A copy needs all five, tested together (366 contract tests, 25 circuit tests).

10 · Business model
Business model

Paid when capital moves, not before.

Planned · not yet in the contracts
01 · PRIMARY

Protocol fee on financed volume

30 to 50 bps of each facility's drawn amount, taken inside the settlement waterfall the contract already runs. No fee if nothing is drawn.

Reference facility: 40,000 USDG drawn × 40 bps = 160 USDG to the protocol; the financier's own fee (3% here, 1,200 USDG) is set per facility.
02 · RISK

Share of cover premiums

Insurers write default and parametric cover through the CoverPool; CargoFlow takes a share of the premium. The insurer keeps the risk; the evidence trail prices it.

Built today: default cover pays min(cover, drawn principal); parametric cover pays after N failed epochs, proven on chain.
03 · SOFTWARE

SaaS for financiers and data

Portfolio risk, exposure and Monte Carlo of default and recovery, evidence API and MCP access for credit funds, insurers and logger vendors; seat or volume pricing.

Built today: Python SDK (portfolio, VaR / ES), TypeScript SDK, remote MCP, OpenAPI 3.1 reference, Dune queries.
Non-custodial by design. The ReceivableVault is the sole USDG custodian and pays out only on the controller's rules. CargoFlow software never holds client funds; financiers lend, insurers insure.
Honest today. The contracts charge no protocol fee yet. Adding it is a waterfall change that ships with the audited mainnet release (milestone M3), alongside work with licensed financiers.

Make existing loggers finance-ready

@cargoflow/gateway already signs and sends logger exports (folder watch, USB, serial, offline queue). Vendor presets for Sensitech, Elitech and ELPRO exist and are marked experimental until checked against real exports.

DOOR 2 · CREDIT FUNDS AND FINANCIERS

Short-tenor USDG yield with evidence attached

The market lists open requests with a suggested fee band; the Python SDK, MCP server and settlement certificates give underwriters the evidence trail per facility.

DOOR 3 · EXPORTERS VIA FORWARDERS AND INSURERS

Onboard through who they already use

Freight forwarders bring shipments; insurers bring parametric cover. The exporter wizard's Pharma template fills 2 to 8 °C, humidity and shock in one click.

Pilot target3 exporters, 1 financier, 1 logger vendor on this lane; then more lanes and cold-chain categories.
